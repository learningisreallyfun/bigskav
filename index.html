Task 7

Water, nitrogen, and carbon cycles

Interactive steps and animated arrows explain how elements move through the system.

Tip: click steps to emphasize routes
Water cycle
  1. Huge rainfall and coastal fog feed roots and streams.
  2. Transpiration fuels fog and local rainfall patterns.
  3. Rivers export water to the Pacific, then evaporation completes the cycle.
Nitrogen cycle
  1. Fixation by bacteria and alder symbionts add bioavailable N.
  2. Plants uptake N; herbivores concentrate it.
  3. Decomposers return N to soil or atmosphere.
Carbon cycle
  1. Photosynthesis stores carbon in wood and soil.
  2. Respiration and decomposition return CO₂ to the air.
  3. Salmon bring ocean carbon inland when they die.
Task 11

Human impacts & conservation

Logging, habitat fragmentation, and invasive species (barred owl) are major pressures. Actions: protect old-growth, maintain riparian buffers, restore salmon runs, and manage invasive competitors.

Logging impacts
Removes nesting trees, increases stream temperatures, and reduces carbon storage.
Conservation options
Riparian protections, selective harvesting, old-growth reserves, salmon restoration.
Community science
Monitoring streams and owls helps target on-the-ground actions.
